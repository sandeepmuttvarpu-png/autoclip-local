AITA For Towing My Neighbor's Luxury Car From My Assigned Parking Spot?

I live in a downtown condominium where every unit gets one deeded, paid parking spot. For the past three months, my wealthy neighbor who owns three sports cars has been using my parking spot as his personal overflow lot. Every time I confronted him, he laughed in my face and said, "Park on the street, kid, you drive a beat-up Honda anyway, nobody cares."

I warned him politely three separate times and even left a printed notice on his windshield. Yesterday, I came home from a twelve-hour hospital shift in the pouring rain, exhausted to my bones, only to find his brand new Porsche parked squarely across my spot. I was done being nice.

Instead of knocking on his door, I called the heavy-duty commercial towing company contracted by our HOA. Within twenty minutes, the flatbed arrived and hauled his luxury car forty miles away to an impound lot in the next county. Because it was an all-wheel-drive vehicle and they had to use dollies, plus the weekend holding fee, it cost him nearly fourteen hundred dollars to get it back. Now he's threatening to sue me. AITA?
