AITA For Cancelling My Sister's Wedding Venue After What She Did?

My sister was getting married, and because our parents couldn't afford a fancy venue, I decided to be the generous older brother and pay for her dream venue out of my own savings. It cost me over twelve thousand dollars. The only condition I had was that I could bring my fiancée, who has been battling cancer, and that she would have a quiet seat near the exit in case she felt sick.

Fast forward to two weeks before the wedding. My sister calls me and says my fiancée isn't allowed to come anymore. When I asked why, she hesitated and then said, "Honestly, her losing her hair and looking sick is going to ruin my wedding photos and take the attention away from me. You can still come alone though!"

I was in absolute shock. I didn't yell. I just hung up the phone. Thirty minutes later, I called the venue manager, gave them my contract number, and cancelled the entire reservation. Since I paid in full, the venue gave me an eighty percent refund. The next morning, my phone blew up with fifty angry calls from my family calling me petty and heartless. So tell me, AITA?
